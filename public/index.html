⚙️ Complete System Flow & Protection Architecture

🛡️ Anti-Fake Order & Cash Management

Closed Ecosystem: Only authenticated restaurant owners can register authorized delivery riders and customers. This prevents bot spam and fraudulent orders.

Pathao-style Float Rule: Delivery personnel must declare their cash in hand. If their balance is lower than the order total, they cannot accept the order unless the restaurant has specifically granted them Credit Collection Privileges.

⏰ 7-Hour Food Reminder & 8-Digit OTP

Food Item Follow-up: Daily food items can run out anytime. Every 7 hours (configurable in settings), a popup prompts the kitchen: "Are these food items still available?"

Accidental Click Prevention: When food is ready, the rider receives an 8-digit OTP via Web push & WhatsApp. When accepting the job, an OTP input box pops up to ensure conscious acceptance.
